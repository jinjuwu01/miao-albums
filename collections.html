MANUSCRIPT COPIES / 作品與藏本

追尋散佚各地的苗圖 Tracing Miao Albums Across Collections

同一類苗圖可能有多種傳抄本、不同題名和各異的圖文編排。這份雙語目錄將「作品／傳本系統」與「實際館藏版本」分開記錄，提供藏所、館藏號、線上影像與待核事項，不把同名抄本誤認為同一原件。

Miao albums survive in different manuscript copies, titles and page arrangements. This bilingual catalogue distinguishes a work or provisional textual family from a particular physical copy. It links verified sources while flagging uncollated versions and incomplete metadata.
